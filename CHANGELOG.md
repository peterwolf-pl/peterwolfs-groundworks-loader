## [0.1.0+26.3] - 2026-10-08

### Added
- Initial release of **Peterwolf's Groundworks Loader** for Minecraft 26.3 Fabric.
- Heavy front-end articulated wheel loader for earthmoving and stockpiling.
- High-capacity front scoop bucket with dual hydraulic lift and tilt arm kinematics.
- Integration with Groundworks volumetric world containers and granular terrain heaps.
- Custom diesel engine loop, working horn (`C`), and dual exhaust smoke particles.
